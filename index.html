Before you message me

Common questions
I don't know exactly what I need yet — is that okay?

Yes. Most projects start as "this spreadsheet is a mess" or "we keep missing compliance deadlines," not a finished spec. Send me what's going wrong and I'll turn it into a clear, scoped deliverable before any work starts.

Do you work in Excel, Google Sheets, or both?

Both — I'll build in whichever your team already uses, so there's no new software to adopt.

Is this a one-time build or ongoing help?

Either. The first three services are project-based with a clear handoff; the back-office tier is for owners who'd rather have it managed continuously.

What if my business isn't in scrap or industrial services?

That background is where the approach comes from, not the limit on who it's for — the same dashboard, compliance, and HR problems show up in almost any small business.
